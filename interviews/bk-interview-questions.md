# BK Restaurant Client Interview Questions

## 1. Business Background

1. What is your main goal for introducing a new management system?
2. What are the main challenges with managing the restaurant manually?
3. How are restaurant operations currently managed on a daily basis?
4. What are the biggest challenges in managing multiple branches?

## 2. Order Handling

5. How are customer orders currently received and recorded?
6. What problems usually occur during order handling?
7. How are orders communicated to the kitchen staff?
8. How are order changes or cancellations handled?

## 3. Delivery Management

9. How are deliveries currently assigned to delivery riders?
10. What challenges occur during the delivery process?
11. How is delivery status currently monitored or communicated?

## 4. Inventory Management

12. How is inventory currently tracked and updated?
13. What common problems occur in stock management?
14. How are stock shortages handled?
15. How do branches request inventory items from suppliers?

## 5. Employee Management

16. How are employee schedules and shifts managed?
17. How is employee attendance currently recorded?
18. What difficulties exist in managing employees?

## 6. Business Requirements & Expectations

19. What are your main requirements for the new system?
20. What is your definition of done for this project?
21. What would make you consider the system successful after implementation?
